How does the auto scan work?

Your browser shares your screen size, pixel density, refresh rate, memory and CPU core count. We use these to estimate how powerful your phone is. Nothing leaves your phone.

Why do I still pick my model?

Browsers do not always reveal the exact model name, and iPhones hide it completely. Confirming your model makes the sensitivity more accurate.

Choose "My model is not listed" and pick the class that matches your phone: entry, mid range, upper mid or flagship. You still get a full sensitivity.
